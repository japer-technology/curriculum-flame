### The Meaning Behind the Logo

The flame represents the enduring spark of learning, the point where curiosity becomes understanding and knowledge begins to grow.

Its upward movement reflects progress, discovery, and the learner’s natural desire to reach beyond what they already know. Yet the flame is not uncontrolled. Its smooth, continuous form suggests guidance, protection, and purpose: learning encouraged within boundaries designed to preserve safety, integrity, and trust.

The single unified shape reflects a system in which curiosity, policy, privacy, and human oversight work together rather than compete. It represents technology that does not extinguish the desire to learn, nor simply provide every answer, but helps direct that desire toward meaningful and independent understanding.

At its heart, the logo symbolises a flame carefully kept alive—protected enough to endure, guided enough to grow, and free enough to illuminate the learner’s own path.
